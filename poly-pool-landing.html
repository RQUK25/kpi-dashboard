Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about poly pools and working with PoolStore

How long does a poly pool take to install? ▼
Most poly pool projects are completed within 2–3 weeks from groundworks starting. The Polypool interlocking panel system is exceptionally fast — a 10m × 5m pool shell can be assembled in under half a day. The rest of the time is spent on groundworks, plumbing, electrics, and finishing.
Why are poly pools better at retaining heat? ▼
Polypool panels achieve a U-value of just 0.18 W/m²K — that's 8 times more thermally efficient than standard double glazing. This means your pool heats up faster, holds its temperature longer, and costs significantly less to run. It also means you can extend your swimming season well into autumn.
Do I need planning permission for a swimming pool? ▼
In most cases, an outdoor swimming pool in England & Wales falls under permitted development and does not require planning permission, provided it meets certain conditions (not in designated land, not more than half the garden area, etc.). Our advisers will review your specific situation during your free consultation and guide you on any permissions needed.
Can I use a poly pool all year round? ▼
Absolutely. The exceptional thermal efficiency of poly panels means many of our customers swim from early spring through to late autumn outdoors. With a good heating system and a quality pool cover, year-round use is entirely achievable. Indoor poly pools, of course, can be used 365 days a year.
