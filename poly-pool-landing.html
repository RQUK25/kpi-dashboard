Common Questions

Everything you need to know before your consultation.

A poly pool kit includes all the insulated polymer panels to form the pool shell, plus flow fittings, pipework, filtration system, liner, and pool cover. You can also add heating, LED lighting, a roller cover, and chemical starter packs — our expert will walk you through exactly what suits your project.
The unique interlocking panel system means a 10m × 5m pool structure can be assembled in under half a day. For a typical outdoor build, most customers are swimming within 2–3 weeks of delivery. Indoor builds may take a little longer depending on the building work involved.
In most cases, outdoor swimming pools in domestic gardens don't require planning permission under permitted development rights — but this depends on your property, location, and local authority rules. Indoor pools may require building regulations approval. Our experts will advise you on this during your free consultation.
Standard poly pool panels support a constant water depth of 1 metre. For greater depth, a hopper or wedge shape can be built into a reinforced pool floor — our team can advise on the best approach for your needs.
Absolutely. Polypool panels are designed for both indoor and outdoor installations. The exceptional U-value of 0.18 W/m²K actually exceeds the UK Building Regulations Part L requirement for indoor pools (0.25 W/m²K), making it the perfect choice for pool rooms and leisure facilities.
